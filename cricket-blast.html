About Cricket Blast

Woolworths Cricket Blast is the nationwide entry-level program for kids aged 10 and under, and the ideal way to begin a cricketing journey with your local RDCA club. Action-packed weekly sessions of 60–90 minutes teach the skills of batting, bowling and fielding through fun, modified games — in a safe, welcoming and inclusive environment.

There's nothing to bring but enthusiasm: equipment is supplied, every participant receives a Cricket Blast Pack, and parents and carers are encouraged to jump in and join the fun. Players progress through three levels, building confidence and preparing for junior club cricket.
